# 02 — MOSFET and CMOS

**Status:** Study plan.

## Concepts to master
- MOS capacitor, threshold voltage and channel formation
- MOSFET cutoff, linear/triode and saturation regions
- Transfer and output characteristic curves
- CMOS inverter switching and noise margins
- Transistor sizing, delay and power basics

## First experiment
Simulate NMOS drain current versus drain-source voltage at several gate voltages using LTspice or ngspice. Save the circuit, device model, plotted axes, and assumptions.

## Verify
Compare trends with equations and explain deviations due to the model.
